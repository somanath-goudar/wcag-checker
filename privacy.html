Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 May 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how WCAG Checker ("we", "the tool") handles your information at wcag-checker.vercel.app.

1. Your HTML Is Never Uploaded

All accessibility analysis runs entirely in your browser using the built-in JavaScript DOMParser API. The HTML you paste is never sent to any server. You can disconnect from the internet after loading the page and the tool will still work.

2. No Personal Data Collected

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. No account, email address, or login is required.

3. Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics (Google Analytics with IP anonymisation) to understand aggregate usage. Data is anonymous and cannot identify individual users.

4. Advertising

This site may display Google AdSense advertisements. Google may use cookies to serve personalised ads. You can opt out at Google Ads Settings.

5. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are reflected on this page with a revised date.
